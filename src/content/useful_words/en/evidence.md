---
term: Evidence
pronunciation: /ˈev.ɪ.dəns/
definition: >-
  The material you bring from the text or the world to support your claim. In
  English essays, evidence is usually a quoted passage plus the analysis that
  shows why the passage supports your argument. Quoting without analysing
  doesn't count.
example: >-
  A claim without evidence is an opinion. A quotation without analysis is
  decoration. The two belong together.
lang: en

Students often stop at the quote: "As Shakespeare writes, 'X'." That's half the work. The other half is the sentence that follows, where you explain what the quote is doing and why it belongs in your argument.

---
term: Paraphrase
pronunciation: /ˈpær.ə.freɪz/
definition: >-
  Restating an idea in your own words, with the meaning intact and the language
  different. Paraphrasing proves you've understood the original; copying
  doesn't.
example: >-
  Original: 'The quality of mercy is not strain'd.' Paraphrase: 'Mercy can't be
  forced.'
etymology: >-
  From Greek paraphrasis, 'a way of speaking near' or 'alongside.' The word
  imagines your language walking next to the original.
lang: en

A good rule: if you couldn't explain the paraphrased sentence to a thirteen-year-old, you haven't paraphrased it yet. You've just reshuffled.

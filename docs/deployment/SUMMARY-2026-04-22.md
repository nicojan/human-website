## Decisions I locked in — redirect if you disagree

1. **Hugo for the blog** (not Astro). Toolchain-lighter, one binary, easier for editing posts by dropping markdown files.
2. **`/writing/`** as the blog URL (matches your nav label).
3. **`/zh-hant/`** as the ZH URL prefix (not `/zh/`) — precise for Traditional Chinese.
4. **Cloudflare Pages** for the website (matches your CF Tunnel pattern).
5. **`contact@forhuman.ca`** as the sole email across the site (old `hi@forhuman.ca` is gone).
6. **Footer tagline** changed to `Built slowly, one student at a time. · 慢慢打磨，一次一位學生。` — humanized from the original "Made with care, not haste."
7. **Belief block headline** humanized from `Learning is the point. Not the scores.` to `Learning itself is the point.` — the old phrasing is the `x, not y` construction the humanizer flags as a strong AI tell.
8. **ZH `/writing/` links point at the EN blog** until v2 of the blog ships with Chinese posts (the copy doc defers ZH blog to v2).
9. **NDJSON append-log** for contact submissions instead of adding SQLite — keeps the dashboard dep surface unchanged.
10. **No email notifications on contact submissions** — check `/admin/contact` or click Reply to start an email thread. Adding a webhook or SMTP is a follow-up (notes in the runbook).

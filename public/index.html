What We Believe

Learning itself is the point.

In the age of AI, the most important question in education has changed. It's no longer what children learn, but how they stay human while learning. When answers are instant, the muscle that builds real thinking weakens. We teach so that muscle gets stronger.

我們的信念

學習本身，才是重點。

在 AI 的時代，關鍵已經不是孩子學什麼，而是在學習裡怎麼保有自己。答案隨手可得時，思考反而容易偷懶。我們教的，就是把那塊肌肉重新鍛鍊回來。

How We Teach · 我們的教學

Four Principles, with Evidence

01
Principle · 原則

Curiosity over compliance.

以好奇為先。

We connect classwork to the world beyond the page. We watch short clips and examine reliable websites to build context. Students test claims, compare sources, and apply new ideas to issues that matter to them.

02
Principle · 原則

Close reading, thinking out loud.

細讀，也讓思考出聲。

We annotate literature and graphic novels together, modelling how to notice, question, and connect. Students turn careful observation into clear argument, and learn to do the same work on their own.

03
Principle · 原則

Progress you can see.

看得見的進步。

We track every week on a shared Kanban board. Goals, samples of work, and next steps are visible to parents and students. Learning becomes a process, not a grade at the end of term.

04
Principle · 原則

Research habits that travel.

帶得走的研究習慣。

We use current research tools and academic databases to plan, narrow, and verify. Students learn to manage sources, cite responsibly, and write with evidence, well before university asks them to.
